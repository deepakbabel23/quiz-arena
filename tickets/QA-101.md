# QA-101 · Streak bonus

**Type:** Story · **Size:** S · **Used in:** Day 1, Labs 2.1 and 2.2

## Story
As a player, I want a bonus for answering several questions correctly in a row,
so that I'm rewarded for consistency.

## Notes from the product owner
- After 3 correct answers in a row, each further correct answer in the streak earns +50.
- A wrong answer resets the streak.
- The answer response should tell the player their current streak.
- Open question: should a too-slow (past the time limit) correct answer keep the streak alive?
