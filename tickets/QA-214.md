# QA-214 · 50/50 lifeline

**Type:** Story · **Size:** M · **Used in:** Day 1, Module 4 (Spec Kit + ATDD)

## Story
As a player stuck on a hard question, I want to remove two wrong answers once per game,
so that I have a better chance of answering correctly.

## Notes from the product owner
- One 50/50 per game.
- Using it on a question leaves the correct answer plus exactly one wrong answer.
- Using it should cost something; the team suggested halving the points for that question.
- The front end needs a "50/50" button that disables itself after use.
- Open questions: what happens if a question only has two choices? Can it be used after the
  answer was already submitted?
